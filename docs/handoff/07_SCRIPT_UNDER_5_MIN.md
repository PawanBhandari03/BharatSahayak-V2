# 07 - Pitch script, under 5 minutes (about 4:00)

Two speakers: **P** = PawanSingh, **R** = Rahul. About 550 words at a relaxed pace. Slides refer to the 7-slide deck. Do not rush; the pauses are part of the timing.

---

**[Slide 1 - 0:20]**
**P:** Good morning, respected mentors and judges. We are Team Binary-Builders, building in Generative AI.
**R:** I am Rahul, this is Pawan, and our project is **BharatSahayak**: an AI companion that tells every Indian which government benefits they already qualify for.

**[Slide 2 - 0:35]**
**P:** India spends over fifteen lakh crore rupees on welfare every year, yet about **2.6 lakh crore goes unclaimed**.
**R:** Meet Savitribai, 52, a widow farmer in Osmanabad. She qualifies for around nine schemes worth about 43,000 rupees a year. She has claimed zero, because no one told her.
**P:** Not a money problem. An information problem.

**[Slide 3 - 0:30]**
**R:** Our solution: the citizen speaks or messages in their own language, gives a few details, and our AI engine checks them against scheme rules.
**P:** They get a personalised list, with the amount, documents needed and deadlines. It works for students, professionals, entrepreneurs and citizens.

**[Slide 4 - 0:45]**
**R:** Five features. A **zero-internet call bot**, so a basic phone is enough. **Deadline alerts**, because missed deadlines are the biggest reason benefits go unclaimed.
**P:** **Life Timeline**: when Savitribai's daughter turns eighteen, the scholarship alert reaches her. A **WhatsApp bot with a voice PIN**. And a **Benefit Wallet** showing received, available, pending and future benefits.

**[Slide 5 - 0:55]**
**P:** How it works. A message or call reaches Twilio, which passes it to our Node.js backend. An AI model handles the conversation in Hindi, Marathi or English.
**R:** The eligibility itself comes from a **rules engine over a verified scheme dataset**, so answers are consistent and checkable. Profiles are stored in a database, and the web dashboard shows the same results.
**P:** Where do the schemes come from? A curated, verified dataset with official source links. For scale we will use official data channels, with human review of every scheme.

**[Slide 6 - 0:25]**
**R:** If you have a phone number, you can use it: no smartphone, no internet. We project about one lakh thirty-seven thousand rupees of benefits found per user. That is a projection, which our pilot will test.

**[Slide 7 - 0:30]**
**P:** The citizen is always free. We earn from state departments, CSC operators, CSR sponsors and training providers who gain when eligible people claim.
**R:** Our vision: every citizen informed, every opportunity accessible. We would love your feedback, especially on which district to pilot first. Thank you.
